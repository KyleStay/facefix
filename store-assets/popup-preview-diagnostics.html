How refresh protection works

Returning to Facebook: Facebook may refresh in response to focus or visibility changes. Facefix intercepts those signals and blocks cancelable automatic reloads and Home redirects while you are away and for 4 seconds after you return.

Feed changes inside the page: The feed can move without a full reload. For up to 15 seconds after returning, Facefix tries to keep the same visible post in place, if it is still loaded.

Idle tabs: Chrome can unload an idle tab and reload it when opened. Facefix asks Chrome to keep Facebook tabs in memory. It cannot prevent every forced eviction or crash.

Your actions: Clicking, scrolling, or typing in Facebook ends the short protection windows. Back and manual Reload remain available. When a timer reaches zero, Facefix stops that guard; it never schedules a reload.

These are Facefix's timers. Facebook's internal refresh timers are not exposed, and some internal feed updates can still happen.
